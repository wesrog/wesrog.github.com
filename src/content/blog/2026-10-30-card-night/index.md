Back in 2008 I posted a screenshot of [a game I loved](/blog/2008-08-12-i-love-this-game/):
Mondo Solitaire on the original iPhone. Dozens of solitaire games on a wooden table,
each variant listed with its rules and your win/loss record right there. I played
a lot of Streets and Alleys.

Mondo Solitaire is long gone, so we built our own. It's called
[Card Night](/cardnight/), and it's for iPhone and iPad.

The lobby is a nod to Mondo: a wooden table, an info panel up top, and one row
per variant with the variant in brackets, like "Klondike [Vegas]" or
"Streets and Alleys [Up and Down]". Even the castle-style games fan their rows
out to the right the way Mondo did.

There are 18 solitaires (Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Streets and Alleys, Yukon,
Canfield, Pyramid, TriPeaks, Golf and more), plus Hearts, Spades, Euchre,
Oh Hell, Crazy Eights, Yaniv, Gin Rummy, War and Cribbage. The table games can
be played against the computer or with friends over Game Center, and the
computer fills any empty seats.

A few other things I like:

- A **Daily Deal**, the same deal for everyone each day, with a streak.
- **Winnable deals only**, a small solver checks each new deal so you never get
  stuck with an impossible one.
- **Undo to last good spot** when you run out of moves.

It's free, with a small banner in the lobby and an occasional ad between games,
never during play.
