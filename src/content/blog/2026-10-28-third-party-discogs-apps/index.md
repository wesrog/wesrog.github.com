Discogs is the database everyone uses, but plenty of collectors would rather
browse it through something else. Here's a running list of the unofficial apps, web tools, browser extensions and self-hosted projects built on
the Discogs API. None of them are affiliated with Discogs.

The official app, for reference, is [Discogs for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1036449551) /
[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.discogs.app).

### Collection clients

Full replacements for the official app: browse, search, and edit your collection and wantlist.

- **[Discographic for Discogs](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/discographic-for-discogs/id979196369)** — Long-running, very complete client. Folders, custom fields, full offline cache, spreadsheet export, quick marketplace listing, plus Apple Music/Spotify playback and Last.fm scrobbling. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_
- **[Wax & Spindle](https://hunted.space/product/wax-spindle)** — Artwork-first client that stores your collection on the device for fast, offline browsing. Wantlist shows lowest current asks. No ads, subscriptions or tracking. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[SnapVinyl for Discogs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741057688)** — Clean client with barcode and OCR text recognition for adding records, automatic translation of release notes, and a random pick button. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[My Vinyl+](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1547173908)** — Renders each record as a virtual disc matching its real vinyl color. Collection value trends, Record Store Day browser, home-screen widgets, and listing to the marketplace. Has its own subreddit, r/MyVinyl. _(iOS, Mac · Free + subscription)_
- **[NeedleDrop](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760035957)** — Collection viewer tuned for iPad, with landscape layouts and high-res cover art. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_
- **[Digs for Discogs](https://apps.apple.com/app/digs-for-discogs/id6760368825)** — Sync once, browse offline forever. Folder browsing and a random album picker. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[On Rotation](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756587007)** — Minimal, fast collection browser. No ads or subscriptions. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[FreshCogs](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6446179457)** — Lightweight client for viewing your Discogs collection. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[Vinyl Crate](https://apps.apple.com/app/vinylcrate/id6754758604)** — Collection stats beyond value: rarity scores from community data, breakdowns by artist/year/genre. Data stays on device; Discogs sync is optional. _(iOS, Mac · Free)_
- **[Vinyl Record App](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1114331735)** — Collection and wantlist with a social feed of what friends are adding. Two-way Discogs sync and a random record widget. _(iOS · Free + $3.99/mo)_
- **[Groovv](https://alternativeto.net/software/groovv/)** — Cross-platform collection app that pairs with Discogs: barcode scanning, condition tracking, and visual browsing. One of the few current options on Android. _(iOS, Android)_
- **[Disko for Discogs](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9nblggh4wdr2)** — Windows client for searching Discogs and managing wantlist, collection and lists, with Spotify lookup. _(Windows)_
- **[Spinner for Discogs](https://snapupdates.popey.com/snap/spinner-for-discogs)** — Linux desktop client (snap). Browse collection and wantlist, spin a wheel for a random album, and control Spotify or YouTube Music. _(Linux · Free · MIT)_

### Value & market tracking

Treat the collection like a portfolio.

- **[WaxTracker](https://waxtracker.app/)** — Reads your collection like a stock portfolio: daily market prices, P&L against what you paid, weekly movers, sales history, and a buy score for wantlist items. _(Web)_
- **[Wax](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760012383)** — Catalog with condition grading and listen logging; the Pro tier adds live value estimates from marketplace data plus CSV/PDF export. _(iOS · Free + Pro)_
- **[Deadwax (pressing guide)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762251685)** — Focuses on which pressing you own and whether it's a keeper, with badges for notable pressings. Not to be confused with the DeadWax spin tracker below. _(iOS)_

### Listening, spins & random pickers

For deciding what to put on next, and logging what you played.

- **[DeadWax (spin tracker)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760111602)** — Imports your collection and logs each play to build a listening history. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_
- **[SpinRoom for Discogs](https://mlpsoftware.itch.io/spinroom-for-discogs)** — Browse by album, artist or song; a random picker that can favor records you haven't played lately; Genius bios and lyrics while it spins. _(Mobile)_
- **[Too Many Records!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6449257927)** — Does one thing: picks a random record from your collection, with format filters. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[DiscoJanice](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6740820977)** — Random album picker with Siri Shortcuts. Collects no data. _(iOS, Mac · Free)_
- **[Record Selektah](https://palmaradio.substack.com/p/the-record-selektah)** — Local Python app that picks your next record semi-randomly, weighting favorites and rotating decades and genres, with listening stats on the side. _(Self-hosted · Free)_

### Digging & scanning

Built for the record store: scan a barcode, check the price, avoid duplicates.

- **[Vinyl – Scan and see the info](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1433818198)** — Simple digging companion: scan, see the pressing, compare prices. Built without trackers or ads. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_
- **[Scan My Records](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6476490241)** — Barcode-first adding to collection and wantlist, pitched at both collectors and record shops. _(iOS · Free)_
- **[VinylBox](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1212071750)** — Collection tracking plus selling: list to the marketplace with price suggestions and manage listings. Price-tracking widget. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_

### DJ tools

- **[kallax](https://apps.apple.com/app/kallax/id6758233437)** — For vinyl DJs: tags your collection with BPM and builds sets sorted by tempo, genre or mood. _(iOS · Free + IAP)_

### Web tools & browser extensions

- **[Discogs Enhancer](https://addons.mozilla.org/addon/discogs-enhancer/)** — The power-user browser extension: dark mode, price comparisons and currency conversion, multi-column collection sorting, charts and stats. Chrome and Firefox. _(Extension · Free + Pro)_
- **[Waxlog](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/waxlog)** — Crate-digging style collection explorer with stackable filters and grouping, plus shareable curated lists. _(Web · Free + premium)_
- **[Discogs collection visualizer](https://cables.gl/p/sOc9Zr)** — Quick experiment that tiles a user's whole collection as thumbnails. _(Web · Free)_

### Self-hosted & open source

Run it yourself. Handy if you want to own your data or hack on it.

- **[Home Assistant Discogs integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/discogs/)** — Sensors for collection and wantlist counts, plus a random record suggestion you can surface on a dashboard. _(Self-hosted · Free)_
- **[discogs-mcp](https://github.com/rianvdm/discogs-mcp)** — MCP server so AI assistants can query your collection. Deploys to the Cloudflare Workers free tier. _(Self-hosted · Open source)_
- **[Discogs Copilot](https://github.com/WOIII-me/Discogs-MCP)** — Another MCP server, with a companion Chrome extension and a hosted instance. _(Self-hosted, Extension · Open source)_
- **[vinyly](https://github.com/mttwhlly/vinyly)** — SvelteKit app that plays your Discogs collection through Spotify (Premium needed). _(Self-hosted · Open source)_
- **[DVinyl](https://github.com/Kyonew/Dvinyl)** — Dockerized collection manager for records and CDs, plus books, movies and games. _(Self-hosted · MIT)_
- **[discogs-record-collection](https://github.com/mgrsskls/discogs-record-collection)** — Grant access and get a simple overview of everything you own. _(Self-hosted · Open source)_
- **[TheCollection](https://github.com/NunoCPNP/thecollection)** — Svelte web app for browsing a collection via the Discogs API. _(Self-hosted · Open source)_
- **[oh-my-collection](https://github.com/thiago-scherrer/oh-my-collection)** — GitHub Action that backs up your collection automatically. _(Self-hosted · Open source)_

### Seller & shop tools

Inventory sync and storefront plugins for people selling on the Discogs marketplace.

- **[Disconnect (ConstaCloud)](https://apps.shopify.com/disconnect-by-constacloud-1)** — Syncs listings, orders, pricing and inventory between Discogs and Shopify, WooCommerce or Wix. _(Web · Paid)_
- **[Blocks for Discogs Sellers](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=279240)** — WordPress blocks that embed your seller badge, profile stats, inventory grid, or single listings on your own site. _(Web · Free)_

### Where it all started

Third-party apps that became (or preceded) the official ones.

- **[MilkCrate](https://www.thevinylfactory.com/features/the-official-new-discogs-app)** — Popular indie iOS client whose success shaped the official iOS app in 2016. _(iOS · Defunct)_
- **[Discographer](https://www.androidpolice.com/2016/07/13/official-discogs-app-spins-play-store/)** — Andreas Dahl's Android client. Discogs partnered with him and it became the official Android app. _(Android · Defunct)_
- **[Discollector](https://factmag.com/2015/04/21/discollector-app-android-discogs)** — MilkCrate's Android sibling, one of the first serious Discogs apps on the platform. _(Android · Defunct)_
